The Register

A land registry records who owns which parcel. GECKO records who may run which network in which area. The kit borrows from survey maps and official records: parcels, hatching, corner markers, monospaced entries. Calm, exact, and accountable, never a hacker aesthetic.

Verdicts are the loudest thing

Green and red are reserved for Verified and Conflict. The primary button is ink, and the one brand accent is cobalt, so a verdict never competes with chrome.

Machines print in mono

SSIDs, domains, coordinates, timestamps and certificate fields are set in JetBrains Mono. Everything a person reads is IBM Plex Sans.

Registered means hatched

Any registered area is a cobalt dashed parcel with survey hatching. The reader's phone is cobalt too: you and the register share one colour.

Facts: one leads (Problem)

A network name is just a label anyone can set.

Any laptop can broadcast Skyport Free WiFi. Nothing checks it.

Your phone auto-joins names it has seen before.

The sign-in page can ask for anything.

Email, card details, passwords.

Four-step flow (How it works)

  1. Operator + Geo CARegister

    The airport proves it operates at this address and registers its network.

  2. GECKO mapPublish

    The GeoCert goes into a public map. Entries can't be quietly added or hidden.

  3. Your phoneLook up

    "Which networks are registered where I am?" The answer comes with a proof.

  4. GECKOGuardCompare

    What the phone sees is compared with what is registered.

Where the attacker fails

  1. RegisterStopped

    Can't get a GeoCert for a place it doesn't operate.

  2. PublishStopped

    Can't hide the real registration from the map.

  3. Look upStopped

    Can't forge the answer. The proof wouldn't check out.

  4. CompareFlagged

    Its network doesn't match, so it's flagged as a Conflict.
